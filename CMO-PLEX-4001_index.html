CMO-PLEX-4001: Conduct Civil Information Management Activities

The Performance Evaluation Checklist (PECL) generated from training events within the Marine Corps Training Information Management System (MCTIMS) may not always be inclusive. Evaluators must remember when determining whether or not a unit and/or individual is "Trained," "Partially Trained," or "Untrained," that additional actions may be required to facilitate accomplishment of each event component or performance step.

Condition: Given higher headquarters order, commander's initial planning guidance, CIM systems, and an area of operations.

Standard: Improve the commander's awareness and understanding of the civil component of the operating environment in accordance with JP 3-57 appendix C.
